Huế Citadel · Ngọ Môn

Brief & approach

Translate historic reference into an editable Blender entrance ensemble: gate platform, roof composition, timber, terraces, forecourt and moat context.

My contribution

Project direction and review within an AI-assisted modeling workflow. The scene was built with scripted geometry and materials; this is not a claim of entirely manual modeling.

Cultural storytelling / Blender + Unreal Engine

Saigon Plaza

Explore Vietnamese coffee culture through a café environment, original prop geometry and a real-time project. These images are actual project artifacts, not generated concept art.

My contribution

Cultural direction, production choices, review and testing alongside AI-assisted asset creation and implementation.

Production evidence

Editable Blender assets and FBX exports. Source checks record round-trip dimensions within 0.1 mm and no nonmanifold or degenerate geometry in the three-prop batch.

Where the work stands

The prop checks do not establish final Unreal integration. Materials, collision and engine appearance require their own review. The gallery keeps asset renders and in-engine captures clearly separated.

Production method

From direction to editable work.

Scope → create → inspect

Translate a creative brief into bounded outputs. Use AI-assisted workflows and MCP-connected applications to create native documents, export review copies and inspect the result.

Evidence → handoff

Keep the editable source, representative exports and documented limitations together. SIGNAL / AI provides a concrete example: native design boards, a presentation, speaker notes and inspected exports.

My role is direction, decisions, review and refinement; AI contributes implementation. The portfolio distinguishes completed artifacts from planned capabilities.
